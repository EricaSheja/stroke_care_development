# MVP line

Owners: Erica and Malaika

## Must

| Feature | Acceptance sentence | Built? |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Sign up, sign in, role, consent | Given a new visitor, when they create a survivor or prevention account and accept the consent screen, then they reach their home page. Nobody can sign up as a clinician. | Yes |
| Blood pressure log with history | Given a signed in survivor, when they save two whole numbers, then the reading appears at the top of their history. | Yes |
| Recovery plan | Given a clinician adds a task to a linked patient, when the survivor opens the plan, then the task is listed and can be ticked for today. | Yes |
| Daily check in | Given a survivor, when they answer or skip any of the ten questions and send, then the answers and note are saved with a time. | Yes |
| Education library | Given any signed in user, when they open Learn, then every item shows its source and whether a clinician has reviewed it. | Yes |
| Clinician summary | Given a clinician, when they open Home, then they see counts, averages, latest reading and patient notes for the last 7 days, with no diagnosis. | Yes |

## Should

* English, Kinyarwanda and French for the app screens. Built for screens. Check in questions and education stay English until reviewed translations exist.
* Emergency button that only tells the user to call local emergency services. Built, with no phone number.
* Low bandwidth friendly pages. Partly built: system fonts, no large images. Real testing is Planned.

## Later (backlog, Planned)

Caregiver permissions and dashboard, AI assisted weekly summary, clinician alerts, appointments, reminders, blood pressure schedule set by a clinician, audit logs, deeper health profile and medical history, verified emergency contacts, admin screens.

## Out of scope for Tuesday

Real patients, real data, clinical thresholds, any claim of clinical effectiveness.
